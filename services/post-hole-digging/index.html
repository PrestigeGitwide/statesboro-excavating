Auger Work for Planned Fences, Gates, and Structures

Statesboro Excavating drills post holes for prepared fence lines, gates, pole structures, decks, signs, and similar projects around Statesboro and Bulloch County. Before equipment arrives, the customer or installer establishes the layout, diameter, depth, spacing, and structural requirements.

Access and underground conflicts matter as much as the number of holes. Gates, slopes, overhead clearance, roots, buried utilities, septic components, old concrete, and wet ground can affect whether an auger can reach and drill a marked location.

What Must Be Ready Before Drilling

Every hole should be visibly marked and the route should be cleared enough for equipment. Utility locating must be complete, property lines confirmed, and the installer should specify the hole size required by the design and applicable code.

We drill the agreed holes; post setting, concrete, layout, engineering, inspections, and permits are separate unless specifically included. Unknown obstructions can require relocation, a different method, or a revised scope.

The correct depth depends on the project, post height, soil conditions, and local requirements. Fence posts, deck footings, and pole barn posts may all require different depths.

Can you drill through rocky soil?

Our auger equipment can handle many tough soil conditions, including clay, compacted dirt, and moderate rock. Extremely rocky ground or large underground obstructions may require additional work.

Do you install the posts too?

Our main service is post hole digging and auger drilling. Depending on the project, we can discuss what additional site preparation may be needed.

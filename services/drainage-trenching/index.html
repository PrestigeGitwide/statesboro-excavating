French Drain Installation Trenching

French drains are one of the most common drainage solutions for managing water around homes and outdoor structures. These systems help collect and redirect water away from foundations, low spots, retaining walls, and areas where water tends to collect.

Proper trench depth and slope are important for French drain performance. Home Dig Solutions provides trench excavation for French drain systems in Statesboro and surrounding Bulloch County communities, helping homeowners prepare for improved drainage and water control.

Downspout and Gutter Drainage Trenching

Downspouts that discharge too close to a home or garage can create erosion, foundation moisture issues, and standing water problems. Buried drainage lines and runoff trenches can help move water farther away from the structure.

We provide trenching for buried downspout drains, gutter runoff systems, and water diversion projects that help improve drainage around residential and rural properties.

Frequently Asked Questions About Drainage and Trenching Services

What types of drainage problems can trenching help solve?

Trenching can help improve standing water, muddy yards, runoff control, drainage around foundations, gutter drainage issues, and low spots that collect water after rainfall.

Do you install French drains?

We provide trench excavation and site preparation for French drain systems and drainage improvements depending on the project scope.

Can you trench for water and electrical lines?

Yes. We dig trenches for water lines, electrical service, irrigation systems, utility lines, and underground piping projects.

Can your equipment handle rocky soil?

Our equipment can handle many compacted, soft, or root-filled soil conditions, along with moderate rock. Extremely rocky ground or buried obstructions may require additional work.

Do I need utilities marked before trenching?

Yes. Underground utilities should always be located and marked before trenching begins to help avoid damaging buried lines.

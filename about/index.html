How We Scope a Statesboro Property Project

A useful estimate starts with more than acreage or linear feet. We look at machine access, vegetation density, slope, wet areas, buried-service concerns, disposal expectations, and the finish you need. Photos help with an initial conversation, while complicated grading and drainage projects may require an on-site look.

Before scheduling, property owners should also identify boundaries and verify required approvals. Work involving construction, wastewater, or land disturbance may require review by the City of Statesboro, Bulloch County, or another authority. We can coordinate our scope with your builder or installer, but engineered plans, surveys, utility locating, and permits remain separate unless specifically included.

  • Define the outcome: mowing access, a buildable area, better drainage, or a dependable lane.
  • Confirm the limits: property lines, utilities, septic areas, trees to protect, and access routes.
  • Sequence the work: clear first, remove necessary stumps, shape the site, then place material.
  • Agree on the finish: rough construction grade and lawn-ready finish are different scopes.

That planning helps avoid moving material twice or damaging finished work during the next phase.
